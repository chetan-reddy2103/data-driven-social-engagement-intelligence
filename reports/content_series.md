# Content Series — Relatability Test Set

This creative test set is the subject material for the analytics system. Each concept is designed to test
topic, format, hook and caption-style combinations while keeping the creative theme consistent.

| ID | Topic | Format | Hook | Caption Style | Creative Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS01 | Social Anxiety | Short | Visual | Story | "When you rehearse a simple conversation in your head 10 times." |
| CS02 | Social Anxiety | Short | Text | Confession | "I said no to plans because I was scared I would be awkward." |
| CS03 | Dating | Short | Visual | Question | "Do you ever reread a text because you think you sounded weird?" |
| CS04 | Friendship | Short | Visual | Story | "Growing apart from a friend without having a fight." |
| CS05 | Career Stress | Long | Text | Advice | "The pressure of feeling behind everyone at the start of your career." |
| CS06 | Burnout | Short | Visual | Confession | "When resting makes you feel guilty instead of refreshed." |
| CS07 | Self-Doubt | Short | Text | Story | "Looking confident outside while questioning every decision inside." |
| CS08 | Family Pressure | Long | Text | Question | "How do you handle being compared with someone else's career?" |
| CS09 | College Life | Short | Visual | List | "5 thoughts every student has before an important presentation." |
| CS10 | Social Anxiety | Short | Visual | Question | "What's one social situation you avoid even though you want to join?" |

## Testing objective

The series deliberately varies format, hook and caption style so the A/B framework can estimate which
combinations are associated with stronger engagement, shares, saves and retention.

## Production note

These are original test concepts, not claims about actual audience responses. They can be converted into
short-form videos, carousels or text posts for a real-world experiment.
